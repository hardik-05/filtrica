About Filtrica

Air filtration engineered for facilities where air quality is non-negotiable

Filtrica designs and builds multi-stage air filtration systems for environments where particulate control is a safety and compliance requirement, not an afterthought — pharmaceutical production lines, semiconductor cleanrooms, healthcare facilities and heavy industrial plants.

Every system is built around the same principle: pre-filtration to extend service life, fine filtration to protect the final stage, and HEPA H13/H14 media to deliver verified, cleanroom-grade output — in a housing designed to keep running under continuous industrial duty.

Our team works directly with facilities engineers and procurement teams to specify the right configuration for the airflow, space and compliance requirements of each site.

How It Works

Multi-Stage Filtration Technology

Four engineered stages work in sequence — from ambient intake to cleanroom-grade output.

01

Intake

Draws in ambient air carrying dust, pollen and airborne particulate for treatment.

02

Pre-Filter

Aluminum corrugated media — rigid, heat and moisture resistant, built for industrial strength.

03

Fine Filter

Mini-pleat hot-melt construction packs maximum surface area for high CFM airflow.

04

HEPA Filter Unit

H13/H14 high-efficiency particulate media, delivering cleanroom-grade purity.

05

Purified Outlet

Filtered air is released to industrial and cleanroom-grade purity.
